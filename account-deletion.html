Ingredients2Cook Account Deletion

Ingredients2Cook, developed by RezForgeX Tech, allows users to request deletion of their accounts and associated data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Click the Request Account Deletion button below.
  2. Send your request using the email address registered with Ingredients2Cook.
  3. Include "Account Deletion Request" in the subject.
  4. We will review your request and process the deletion.

You do not need to reinstall the app to request deletion. Never send your password.

Request Account Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

  • Firebase Authentication account information.
  • Username and associated profile information.
  • Saved recipes and favorites.
  • Grocery list data.
  • Reviews and ratings associated with your account.
  • Notifications and other account-associated data.

Data Retention

Account-associated personal data will be deleted after the deletion request is processed, except for information that must be retained for legitimate legal or security reasons.
