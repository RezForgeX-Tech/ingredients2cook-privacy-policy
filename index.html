Ingredients2Cook Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

Ingredients2Cook is a recipe application that helps users discover recipes, save favorite recipes, submit reviews and ratings, send feedback, and generate recipe suggestions using offline ingredient matching.

This Privacy Policy explains how Ingredients2Cook collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the application, we may collect or process:

  • Account information: Name, username, email address, and Firebase user ID.
  • Google Sign-In information: Information such as your name, email address, and profile picture, depending on your Google account and the permissions granted.
  • Reviews and ratings: Recipe reviews, ratings, usernames, and associated user IDs.
  • Favorite recipes: Information identifying recipes saved to your favorites.
  • Feedback: Your feedback message, submission timestamp, experience label, and associated user ID.
  • Notifications: Information associated with in-app notifications and push notifications.
  • Grocery list: Grocery items stored locally on your device.
  • Technical information: Certain technical information may be processed by the services used by the app, such as information needed for authentication, database access, push notifications, or downloading the offline recipe database.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide authentication and account recovery.
  • Display and manage recipe reviews and ratings.
  • Save and manage favorite recipes.
  • Receive and review feedback sent to the administrator.
  • Deliver app notifications.
  • Provide recipes and offline ingredient-matching features.
  • Maintain and improve the application's functionality and security.

We do not sell users' personal information.

3. Third-Party Services

Ingredients2Cook uses third-party services to provide its features. These providers may process information as necessary to operate their services.

  • Firebase Authentication: Account registration, sign-in, and authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore: Storage of certain user profile information.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: Storage of recipe reviews, ratings, feedback, and relevant app data.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Delivery of push notifications.
  • Cloudinary: Hosting and delivery of recipe images and videos.
  • GitHub Releases: Distribution of the downloadable offline recipe database.

These providers handle information according to their services and applicable privacy policies. Their processing may involve technical information such as IP addresses or device-related information.

4. Offline Features and Ingredient Matching

Ingredients2Cook provides offline ingredient matching using a locally stored recipe database. After the database has been downloaded to your device, ingredient matching can work without sending your ingredient inputs to a remote AI service.

The grocery list is also stored locally and can be used offline. Downloading the offline recipe database requires an internet connection and involves GitHub or the infrastructure used to deliver the download.

5. Data Storage and Security

User information is stored using the services described in this policy. Grocery list information is stored on your device.

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Reviews, Ratings, and Feedback Retention

Reviews, ratings, and feedback may be retained to support app functionality, administration, troubleshooting, and improvement.

When an account is deleted, some associated content may remain in the application's databases. For example, reviews, ratings, or feedback may be retained, and certain records may continue to contain an associated user identifier.

7. Account Deletion

Users can delete their accounts through the account deletion option available in the application's settings.

Account deletion removes the associated Firebase Authentication account and applicable account profile information. The grocery list is also deleted as part of the account deletion process.

Some records, including reviews, ratings, favorites, notifications, or feedback, may require separate handling and may not all be removed immediately. Certain records may remain associated with the former account identifier.

For questions about account deletion or retained information, contact us at rezforgextech@gmail.com .

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the third-party service providers listed in this policy to provide app features. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the application's security and rights.

9. Children's Privacy

Ingredients2Cook is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly intend to collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

10. International Data Processing

Third-party service providers may store or process information on servers located in countries other than your country of residence. Data protection laws may differ between countries.
